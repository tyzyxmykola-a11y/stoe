# Scientific boundaries and evidence status

## Defensible architectural claim

The strongest tested SToE-derived hypothesis is narrow: preserving failed reasoning with typed provenance and retrieving it conditional on the current observer state can improve recovery when constraints change.

The completed v9.1 work was a corrective replication on an outcome-known benchmark, not independent confirmation. With local `gemma4:26b`, observer-aware topology scored 18/18 overall and 9/9 on targeted tasks; frozen dense retrieval scored 14/18 overall and 6/9 targeted; the strongest conventional retriever, typed semantic retrieval, scored 15/18 overall and 6/9 targeted. The preregistered targeted difference was +33.3 percentage points, with a family-cluster bootstrap 95% interval of 0.0 to 66.7 points and exact paired p = 0.25.

Three topology-only targeted wins failed when the focal failed IP or critical typed relation was removed, while maintaining the visible-artifact budget. This is strong dependence evidence for the implemented pipeline, not universal causal proof.

## Important limitations

- Only nine independent family clusters were available; the primary interval touched zero.
- The benchmark was designed around changed constraints and retained failures.
- Only one generation model/digest and one completed seeded run were tested.
- The correction occurred after prior outcomes were known.
- Equal artifact counts did not guarantee equal semantic information volume.
- Counterfactual graph removals altered downstream retrieval and were pipeline interventions.

Independent evidence requires unseen tasks, more families, additional models, repeated runs, and a frozen protocol created before outcomes are observed.

## Canonical seed evidence

Native seed, no-seed, and chimera conditions scored 18/18, 16/18, and 13/18 overall, but all were 9/9 on targeted tasks. Differences occurred only on controls. This is compatible with useful native integration on those controls; it does not show that the canonical ontology is necessary for the principal failed-path mechanism or generally superior.

## Claims not established by v9.1

Do not present the experiment as proof of:

- a universal Theory of Everything;
- a universal conservation law for information points;
- universal graph-memory superiority;
- AGI or conscious observer status;
- the canonical seed's metaphysical propositions;
- autoinjection as a novel accepted mapping class.

Mathematically, `f: A → A` is ordinarily an endofunction and its range remains a subset of `A`; `A ∈ A` requires an explicitly chosen non-well-founded foundation. Self-evaluation in an architecture also requires reifying operations, traces, or models as inspectable objects. Treat these as definitions or research proposals unless formal proofs and foundations are supplied.

## Recommended phrasing

Prefer:

> The experiment supports an SToE-derived architectural hypothesis about observer-aware retrieval of conserved failed reasoning on this benchmark.

Avoid:

> The experiment proves SToE or a Theory of Everything.
