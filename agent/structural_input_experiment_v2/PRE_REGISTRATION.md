# Structural-Input Experiment v2 — Preregistration

Status: **FROZEN NOT RUN**

V2 is a corrective new-holdout experiment. It does not reuse any v1 hidden case, family label, answer ref, or item content. Its eight targets cover distinct changed constraints; four controls test still-active rejection bases, measured evidence, and serialization behavior.

Both arms use the same local model/digest, active source, observable diagnostics, content-matched memory, inert policy language, two-call budget, seeds, sampling, gates, and hidden evaluation. The structural arm alone receives the frozen typed/directed overlay. Both candidates must be generated before hidden evaluation.

The v2 proposal uses enumerated mechanism IDs instead of a lexical prose gate. Every string and list is schema bounded. Before these cases were authored, the largest evidence-valid proposal passed both complete mocked pipelines; the worst stage retained 1,792 tokens after output and checkpoint reserves, exceeding the required 1,024-token margin.

Primary criterion: structural passes at least two more of eight changed-constraint targets than ordinary and no fewer controls. Unevaluated metrics are null. A candidate activates only through the pre-existing strict public and hidden gates; ties do not favor SToE.

A positive result is preliminary evidence about this fixed relational-input intervention, model, and policy task—not proof of SToE or general topology superiority.
