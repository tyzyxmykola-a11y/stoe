# ChatGPT escalation

Escalate when an architecture decision is required, hard integration or
debugging remains unresolved, a trust boundary would change, local agents
materially disagree, or local agents cannot resolve a failure.

Conserve local work in SToE before escalation. Send only:

`problem | current_state | evidence | failed_paths | competing_conclusions | constraints | requested_decision`

Keep it compact. ChatGPT is the SToE architecture authority.
